# CamperPower Foundation Strategy

## Executive Thesis
CamperPower should be built as a trust-centered engineering product, not as a generic affiliate content site. The strategic moat is the combination of transparent calculation logic, compatibility intelligence, and safety framing.

## Strategic Position
CamperPower occupies a gap between:
- high-authority but generic informational pages,
- commercial shop-led content,
- fragmented forum advice.

The defensible wedge is a deterministic and explainable tool that maps user intent directly to a technically reasoned bill of materials.

## 6-Lens Synthesis
Based on the PDF extract, the opportunity remains attractive if validated against keyword and partner acceptance data:

1. Market and intent
- Demand appears seasonal but practical and purchase-oriented.
- Core intent is problem-solving under risk, not casual browsing.

2. Competition
- Existing SERP landscape lacks a strong independent NL configurator.
- Many pages answer parts of the problem but not the full decision chain.

3. User journey
- Conversion opportunity concentrates in sizing and compatibility stages.
- Friction is highest where wrong decisions are expensive or unsafe.

4. Monetization
- Hardware basket commissions can work, but program acceptance is critical.
- Tool-led pages can outperform content-only pages in click intent quality.

5. Right-to-win
- Technical background plus ability to build tools creates non-trivial moat.
- Explainability and source-backed logic compounds trust over time.

6. Risk
- Safety and liability are first-order constraints.
- Scope and disclaimer discipline is mandatory, not optional.

## Core Product Doctrine
- Recommendation without explanation is not acceptable.
- Advice without scope boundaries is not acceptable.
- Monetization that degrades neutrality is not acceptable.
- Every rule must be versioned and reviewable.

## Decision Framework
Use this three-gate framework before scaling content:

Gate A: Viability
- Search demand threshold met.
- Affiliate program acceptance threshold met.

Gate B: Reliability
- Rule-engine test coverage and scenario pass rate met.
- Compatibility checker confidence labeling in place.

Gate C: Trust and compliance
- Disclosure, disclaimer, and safety UX implemented.
- Expert review workflow active for high-impact rules.

## Portfolio Strategy
- CamperPower is standalone first.
- EV can be integrated as module in an existing energy brand later.
- GardenTech remains optional and validation-dependent.

## 12-Month North Star
Build the highest-trust Dutch decision platform for camper 12V upgrades, measured by recommendation completion, repeat usage, and outbound conversion quality rather than page count.
