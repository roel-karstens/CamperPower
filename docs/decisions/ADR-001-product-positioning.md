# ADR-001: Product Positioning as Independent Configurator

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The niche analysis indicates a gap for an independent Dutch-language camper electrical configuration tool. Existing options are fragmented across brands, shops, media, and forums.

## Decision
CamperPower will position itself as an independent, explainable decision layer rather than a webshop or generic affiliate blog.

## Consequences
Positive:
- Clear differentiation in SERP and user trust.
- Better fit with tool-led conversion and repeat usage.
- Stronger defensibility through rule engine quality.

Tradeoffs:
- Higher engineering and validation burden.
- Slower content-only scaling versus generic review sites.

## Follow-up
- Enforce explainability for every recommendation output.
- Maintain explicit neutrality and disclosure standards.
